🚀 Aroosh's Metric Adventure

From giant Kilometers (km) down to tiny Millimeters (mm)!

Click on any step of the ladder to inspect how big it is!

🚗
Kilometer (km)
For long car trips & airplane paths
1 km = 1,000 m
⬇️ × 1,000 (Add 3 zeros!)
🚪
Meter (m)
Height of a door or a big step
1 m = 10 dm = 100 cm
⬇️ × 10 (Add 1 zero!)
📱
Decimeter (dm)
About the width of your hand or a phone
1 dm = 10 cm
⬇️ × 10 (Add 1 zero!)
🖍️
Centimeter (cm)
Width of your fingernail or a crayon
1 cm = 10 mm
⬇️ × 10 (Add 1 zero!)
🐜
Millimeter (mm)
Tiny! Thickness of a coin or a small ant
Smallest step here!

👆 Tap any step above!

Every time you step down from m → dm → cm → mm, you multiply by 10. Going from km → m is a giant leap of 1,000!

Aroosh's Breakfast Discovery!

This morning before breakfast, Aroosh spotted a super math secret: 150 mm = 15 cm! Because 10 mm = 1 cm, dividing by 10 (chopping off one zero) turns millimeters into centimeters!
